# HaeSiivooja — SaaS Marketplace, Mobile Apps & AI Search

**Role focus:** SaaS Product Engineering · Web & Mobile · AI Search · Data & AI Platform

## Summary

HaeSiivooja is a Finnish cleaning-services product ecosystem combining a customer-facing SaaS marketplace, native mobile applications and an **AI-powered cleaning company search in Finland**.

The same marketplace domain also supports the companion **Real-Time Marketplace Intelligence & AI Decision Platform**, connecting operational marketplace activity to streaming data engineering, forecasting, ranking, governed metrics and controlled AI-assisted decisions.

**Product → web/mobile SaaS → operational data → AI search → real-time data platform → governed AI decisions**

## Marketplace

Customers can discover cleaning professionals, review service information and pricing, and arrange bookings. Cleaning professionals can maintain their marketplace presence, availability and pricing and receive work opportunities.

[Visit HaeSiivooja](https://haesiivooja.fi/)

## AI-powered cleaning company search

The product ecosystem also includes a dedicated AI-powered search experience for finding cleaning companies in Finland.

[Open AI-powered cleaning company search](https://app.haesiivooja.fi/)

## Native mobile applications

The marketplace includes native mobile applications:

- **Android:** Kotlin
- **iOS:** Swift

## Companion data & AI platform

The companion **HaeSiivooja Real-Time Marketplace Intelligence & AI Decision Platform** extends the same domain into a production-oriented architecture:

**MySQL → Debezium CDC → Kafka → Bronze/Silver/Gold → forecasting and ranking → governed decision API → policy/human approval → marketplace outcomes**

[Read the Real-Time Marketplace Intelligence & AI Decision Platform case](/blog/haesiivooja-ai-marketplace-intelligence)
